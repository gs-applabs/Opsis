# Landmark photos

The landmark sprites of the weather wallpaper are cut-outs of photographs from Wikimedia Commons. Only public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA material is used (no non-commercial, no no-derivatives, no unknown licences). The cut-outs marked CC BY-SA are modified versions (derivatives) and are therefore released under the same CC BY-SA licence; the app code stays MIT. Every photo was checked individually against the Commons licence data on the date below. Changes: the sky was removed, the picture was cropped and scaled, and converted to WebP.
