# Landmark photos

The landmark sprites of the weather wallpaper are cut-outs of photographs from Wikimedia Commons. Only public domain, CC0 and CC BY material is used (no share-alike, no non-commercial, no unknown licences). Every photo was checked individually against the Commons licence data on the date below. Changes: the sky was removed, the picture was cropped and scaled, and converted to WebP.
