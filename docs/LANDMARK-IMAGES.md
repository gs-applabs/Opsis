# Landmark images - licence manifest

Rule (plan 2.1.5, 0C.2 #9): every photo is checked on its own before use. Allowed: public domain, CC0, CC BY (with attribution). NOT allowed: CC BY-SA (share-alike), NC, ND, unknown or fair-use licences. The monument itself must be free of rights (old work or a country whose law allows photographs of it); lit-up night views of protected works are not used.

Machine-readable copy: `docs/LANDMARK-IMAGES.json` (generated by `tools/landmarks/build_sprites.py`, which refuses any licence outside the allowed list).
