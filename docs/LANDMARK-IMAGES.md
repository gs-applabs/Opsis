# Landmark images - licence manifest

Rule (plan 2.1.5, 0C.2 #9): every photo is checked on its own before use. Allowed: public domain, CC0, CC BY 2.0/3.0/4.0 and CC BY-SA 2.0/3.0/4.0 (both with attribution). NOT allowed: NC, ND, unknown or fair-use licences.

**CC BY-SA and the app licence:** a cut-out of a CC BY-SA photo is a modified version (a derivative) of that photo. The modified image files marked CC BY-SA below (arta.webp, corinth.webp) are therefore released under the same CC BY-SA licence (the same version as the original); they are separate image assets, the application code stays MIT. The credit lines (author, licence, source link) are in `landmarks/ATTRIBUTION.md`, `landmarks/manifest.json` (field `attribution`) and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. The monument itself must be free of rights (old work or a country whose law allows photographs of it); lit-up night views of protected works are not used.

Machine-readable copy: `docs/LANDMARK-IMAGES.json` (generated by `tools/landmarks/build_sprites.py`, which refuses any licence outside the allowed list).

Tried and rejected (2026-11-18, quality of the cut-out, not licence): Piraeus Municipal Theatre (street poles, wires, traffic lights in front), Ioannina Aslan Pasha mosque (grey sky is not keyed out), Olympia Temple of Zeus (trees; the other file is a drawing), Larissa theatre (a rectangular slab, no silhouette), Kastoria Koubelidiki (hill and square stay in), Drama Agia Sophia (neighbouring buildings, hard edges), White Tower Thessaloniki (night shot with people and trees), Corfu Old Fortress (fort too small against the sea), Zakynthos bell tower (cloudy sky not keyed), Heraklion Koules and Alexandroupoli lighthouse (clouds, no clear sky). Earlier rejections: Statue of Liberty, Giza pyramids. For the other capitals no Commons photo with an allowed licence and an isolated monument against a clear sky was found; they keep the silhouette.
Total size of the sprites: about 740 KB (WebP). Loaded only when chosen / near the weather location (one image at a time).

## Own silhouettes (no photograph)
All 53 landmarks, including the photographed ones (fallback when a photo cannot be loaded), have our own vector drawing; the landmarks of 45 prefecture capitals are only that. The drawings are our own vector drawings (`landmarks.js`, generated by `tools/landmarks/gen_greek_landmarks.py` from `tools/landmarks/greek_templates.js`), made from the general shape of each monument; no photograph, model or third-party artwork was traced or copied, so they carry the licence of the app (MIT) and need no attribution. The list with the form of every capital is in `docs/LANDMARKS-GREEK-TODO.md`.
