# Opsis origins

Application foundation: Opsis 2.6.0 source and Windows x64 dependencies supplied with this project. See LICENSE, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and src/Widgets/wallpaper/landmarks/ATTRIBUTION.md for upstream notices.

Studio adds its main window layout, grouped navigation and overview, the revised zone and container editors, GIF frame preview and selection, and the in-wallpaper music renderer. The music renderer includes the canvas visualizations from the MIT-licensed Opsis Media Suite, adapted to system loopback audio and the desktop palette. The included Music Suite notice identifies that project's license.

The 12 landscape photographs are individually attributed and licensed in `src/Widgets/wallpaper/scenes/ATTRIBUTION.md` and the adjacent manifest. The other capital scenes are original stylized illustrations rendered in code.

Studio.4 adds an original AI-generated mountain-lake wallpaper and its runtime weather effects. The image is illustrative of an Epirus-style landscape, with no claim that it depicts a specific physical viewpoint.


The four Ancient Athens lighting images were supplied for this edition. They are an artistic reconstruction of the city.

The 52 city backgrounds in `widgets/wallpaper/scenes/greece/` were supplied for this edition.
